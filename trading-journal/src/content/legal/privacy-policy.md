# PRIVACY POLICY — EDGEFLO

Last updated: 10 February 2026

Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how EdgeFlo (“we”, “our”, “us”) collects, uses, and protects your information.

## 1. Information We Collect

We may collect:

### 1.1 Personal Information

- Name
- Email address
- Payment information (handled by Stripe)
- Profile picture (optional)

### 1.2 Usage Data

- Login activity
- Device/browser information
- IP address
- Session duration
- Crash logs

### 1.3 Trading-Related Data

If you connect broker accounts, we may collect trade history, PnL, balance, and metrics used for journaling and analytics.

### 1.4 AI Interactions

Messages typed into FloAI, Notebook, journal entries, and prompts and responses. This data helps improve your account experience and AI accuracy.

## 2. How We Use Your Information

We use your data to provide access to the Platform, display analytics and metrics, improve AI personalization, troubleshoot issues, send important product updates, prevent fraud and misuse, and improve user experience. We do not sell your data.

## 3. Payment Information

Payments are processed by Stripe. We do not store your card details. See [Stripe’s privacy policy](https://stripe.com/privacy).

## 4. AI Processing

Your AI inputs may be securely processed by OpenAI, in-house AI models, and Chatbase integrations to deliver responses, improve accuracy, and personalize your usage. We do not sell or publish your private trading data.

## 5. Data Security

We use encryption in transit and at rest, authentication via Auth0, role-based access controls, and regular audits. However, no system is 100% secure. You use the Platform at your own risk.

## 6. Sharing Your Data

We may share data with hosting providers, AI service providers, market data providers, analytics tools, and customer support systems. We do not sell data to third parties for marketing purposes.

## 7. Your Rights

Depending on your region, you may request access to your data, correction of inaccurate data, deletion of your account, or export of your data. To request, contact [support@edgeflo.com](mailto:support@edgeflo.com).

## 8. Data Retention

We retain your data while your account is active. If you delete your account, we delete or anonymize data unless required by law.

## 9. Cookies & Tracking

We use cookies for authentication, user experience, and analytics. You may disable cookies in your browser, but some features may not work.

## 10. Children

EdgeFlo is not intended for users under 18.

## 11. Policy Updates

We may update this Privacy Policy as needed. The new version will be posted on this page.

## 12. Contact

For privacy questions, email [support@edgeflo.com](mailto:support@edgeflo.com). Company: EDGEFLO PTE. LTD., Singapore.
