# COOKIE POLICY — EDGEFLO

Last updated: 10 February 2026

This Cookie Policy explains how EdgeFlo uses cookies and similar technologies on our website and within our web app.

## What are cookies?

Cookies are small files stored on your device that help websites and apps function properly, remember preferences, and understand how users interact with pages.

## What we use cookies for

We use cookies and similar technologies to:

1. **Essential operations:** Help the site and app work correctly, including security, fraud prevention, and session management.
2. **Preferences:** Remember settings like theme, language, and other user choices.
3. **Analytics:** Understand usage and improve performance, such as which pages are visited and how features are used.
4. **Marketing (if enabled):** Measure ad performance and show relevant content. We only do this where permitted and based on your choices.

## Types of cookies

- **Strictly necessary cookies:** required for core functions and security
- **Functional cookies:** remember preferences
- **Analytics cookies:** help us improve the product
- **Advertising cookies:** used for marketing measurement and targeting (optional)

## Third-party cookies

Some features may involve third parties, such as analytics, support chat, video hosting, or payment providers. These third parties may set their own cookies and have their own policies.

## Your choices

You can control cookies by using our cookie banner or settings (where available) to accept or reject non-essential cookies, or adjusting your browser settings to block or delete cookies. Blocking essential cookies may cause parts of the site or app to stop working.

## Changes to this policy

We may update this Cookie Policy from time to time. We will update the “Last updated” date above.
